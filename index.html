01 About

Where deep research meets real-world scale.

I'm an AI/ML leader with two decades spanning academic research, elite motorsport engineering, top-tier management consulting, and enterprise banking at scale.

Today I serve as Head of AI/ML Solutions for the Retail Product Pillar within DBS's Transformation & Data Group, where I own a regional portfolio of 250+ AI/ML models across four markets, generating more than $100M in economic value annually. I also act as a Deep eXpert for DBS Innovation Group, spearheading the bank's exploration of frontier technologies including Quantum AI.

Before DBS, I was a Principal Data Scientist at QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey & Company, and a Data Scientist at McLaren Applied, where I helped build the data science and engineering modules behind McLaren's F1-grade telemetry analytics platform. My path began in academia as a Research Scientist at Singapore Management University.

Across every chapter the throughline has stayed the same: turning rigorous research into systems that create durable, measurable value.
